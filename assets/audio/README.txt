CUSTOM GAME AUDIO (CONNECTED)

The supplied sounds are connected to Night One:
- weapons/shotgun-fire.mp3: shotgun shot
- footsteps/footsteps.mp3: walking and sprinting steps
- zombies/idle.mp3: zombie idle/proximity voice
- zombies/attack.mp3: detection and attack voice
- zombies/hurt.mp3: zombie hit/death voice
- environment/generator-start.mp3: generator startup
- environment/radio-static.mp3: transmitter/static startup

The sounds are also embedded in js/audio-pack-data.js so they work when Night-one.html is opened directly.
